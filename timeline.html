How to Read This

Events Are Not Conclusions

A timeline entry does not automatically mean wrongdoing occurred. The purpose of this page is to organize records in sequence so readers can see timing, relationships, repeated patterns, and unanswered questions more clearly.

As the investigation grows, entries should connect back to documents, people, entities, properties, and public decisions wherever possible.
