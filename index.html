Murder Creek Media
Compiled from the primary court file (CV-2008-575, complaint & discovery), Alabama Ethics Commission Statement of Economic Interests filings, and reporting by the Alabama Political Reporter, Lagniappe, Alabama Gazette, Fox10, Alabama Public Radio, WSFA, Gulf Coast Media and public federal dockets. The original settlement date appears as both "June 2005" and "June 2006" within the filing; the ~$47,500 / 2010 resolution and several 2024–2026 items rest on news reporting. The November 2025 ethics complaint is sourced to an anonymous tip and is unconfirmed. Shore Acres purchase/sale prices and profit percentages are drawn from recorded transaction amounts as reported; they have not been independently verified against the county deed records here, and property appreciation in this area is common following subdivision approvals. Allegations are allegations: Kennon has denied wrongdoing throughout, no criminal wrongdoing has been publicly established regarding the SEI amendments or the Shore Acres transactions, and the 2005 and 2010 settlements carried no admission of liability. The Board of Education items are drawn from a first-person community explainer post about a May 6, 2026 council meeting and 2025 school-board vacancy process; some dates (e.g. the Stephen Lewis appointment) are approximate and none of this section has been independently cross-checked against official council minutes.
