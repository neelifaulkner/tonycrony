Why This Exists

Not every transaction, approval, or benefit documented here is directly tied to Tony Kennon personally. This project is focused on the larger web of relationships surrounding Orange Beach politics, including public officials, private entities, developers, business interests, property transactions, planning decisions, lawsuits, and records that raise questions about influence and accountability.

Methodology

Built Around Documents

This project relies on public records, official filings, court documents, planning records, property records, and documented source material. Whenever possible, analysis is separated from verified records so readers can review the evidence directly. Connections are documented through records, timelines, ownership history, public actions, and clearly identified source material.
