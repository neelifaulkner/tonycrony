Connection Standards

What “Connected” Means

A person or entity appearing on this page is not automatically accused of misconduct. A connection may mean the person or entity appears in public records, owns or manages a business, signed a document, received an approval, participated in a transaction, appeared in litigation, or is otherwise tied to a documented event.

The purpose of this page is to help readers follow the larger web of relationships without assuming that every connection has the same meaning or significance.
