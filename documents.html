Source Standards

Why Documents Matter

This site is built around primary source material wherever possible. Documents help readers independently review what records show, what has changed over time, and how different events, people, entities, and properties may connect.

As this archive grows, each document should include a short explanation, source attribution, related timeline entries, and connected people or entities.
